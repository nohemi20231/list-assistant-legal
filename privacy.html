Privacy Policy

List Assistant · effective November 5, 2026

List Assistant is a personal shopping-list and to-do assistant that works by text message. It is run by its owner, a sole proprietor, for the owner's own use. The only person it texts is the owner.

What data we collect

  • The owner's mobile phone number.
  • The text of messages the owner sends to the List Assistant number, and of the replies and reminders it sends back.
  • The shopping items, to-dos, reminder times and product links that come from those messages.
  • Delivery status of each text (for example, delivered or undelivered).

Texts from any other phone number are not stored or answered.

How we use it

Only to keep the owner's lists, send confirmations and reminders, and build amazon.com product or search links that the owner opens and buys from personally. The data is not used for advertising or for any other purpose.

Who processes it

  • Twilio sends and receives the text messages.
  • Anthropic receives the text of each incoming message so its AI model can read what the owner asked for.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

How long we keep it

Data is kept until the owner deletes it. The owner can export or erase all of it at any time.

Opting out

Reply STOP to stop all messages; reply START to opt back in. Reply HELP for help.

Contact

Reply HELP to the List Assistant number, or open an issue on the Issues tab of the GitHub repository that hosts this page (list-assistant-legal).
